# Name: Myelin

## The name

**Myelin.** The myelin sheath — the insulating layer around nerve fibers that makes signals propagate fast. Infrastructure, not the agent. It makes Liv work; it is not Liv.

## Why it works

- **Meaning is recognizable across languages:** myelin (EN/SV), mielina (ES), 미엘린/miellin (KO). No translation required, no false friends.
- **No heavy connotations:** unlike Vigil (watchful/military), Soma (body/drug), Axon (product name) — myelin is a clean technical term with no cultural baggage.
- **The metaphor is accurate:** myelin doesn't generate signals, it makes existing signals faster and more reliable. The runtime doesn't think — it routes, caches, and schedules so that thinking happens at the right time, in the right place, with the right context already loaded.
- **Fits the "brain stem" framing:** Myelin is the infrastructure layer of the nervous system. Toybox is the hardware. Myelin is the runtime. Liv (or whoever runs on it) is the mind.

## Naming conventions

- Project directory: `myelin/`
- Elixir namespace: `Myelin` (replaces `AgentRuntime` throughout)
- Hardware: still **toybox** (the Pi 5 — Myelin's substrate)
- The agent: still **Liv** (or whichever personality is loaded)

## Tagline

*The sheath that makes signals propagate fast.*
